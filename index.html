Super 150s Wool

Sourced from Ermenegildo Zegna mills in Biella. Exceptionally fine with a natural drape that resists creasing through the longest days.

Irish Linen

Cool and textural, woven in Co. Down. Ideal for warmer months without sacrificing structure. It breathes beautifully and ages with grace.

Huddersfield Tweed

Woven from British fleece in West Yorkshire. Our heritage tweeds carry both weight and history — cut equally well for country and city.

Cashmere Blend

A whisper-light wool-cashmere blend for our finest travel and evening pieces. Unrivalled softness that only improves with every wear.
