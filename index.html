Bespoke Atelier

Where cloth becomes character

Three appointments. Six to eight weeks. A suit that fits only you and will outlast every trend.

Consultation & Cloth
We begin with a conversation about occasion, preference, and cloth. Over 400 fabrics from the finest mills in England, Scotland, and Italy.
II
Pattern & Baste Fitting
Your pattern is cut by hand. The baste fitting allows our tailor to see the cloth on the body — adjusting silhouette before the final cut.
